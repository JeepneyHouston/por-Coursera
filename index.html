Final Project

Cathedrals in Vienna :)

Below are nine images of nine cathedrals in the city of Vienna, Austria.

Inspired by a new friend I made this past summer—a friend of a close friend visiting the country to attend my close friend's little sister's debut celebration— who told me about beautiful places to sightsee in their home country of Austria and introduced me to Stephansdom, one of the most beautiful cathedrals in the world (according to her (and I tend to agree)), I decided to look up what other cathedrals there were in and around Vienna and decided to include them in this project as a small sort of token of appreciation for the beautiful religious architecture of Austria as well as commemoration of a delightful newfound friendship from a world so different than my own. I wish to give my special thanks to Lara for her kind warmth, immaculate beauty, and sheer and utter cool-ness; for inspiring me to find a subject for this small project, and for inspiring me to be more unafraid of getting to know new people again.
